Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What this app is

"rclone personal Drive sync" is a personal tool used only by its owner to copy files between the owner's own Google Drive and the owner's own server, using the open-source program rclone.

What data the app accesses

The app accesses files and folders in the owner's Google Drive, with the owner's permission, only to upload and download the owner's own files.

How the data is used and stored

Files go directly between Google Drive and the owner's server. The app has no other servers or databases. We do not collect, sell, share or transfer Google user data to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
